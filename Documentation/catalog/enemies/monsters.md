# Monsters Catalog (early design notes)

This catalog groups monster ideas by taxonomy. These are private design notes, not shipped content. Each entry uses a compact stats window with the same field order for easier reading and future expansion.

## Ogroids

### Nekker
            * Category: Ogroid
            * Threat Level: Low
            * Size: Small
            * HP: 18
            * Armor: Thick hide (minor natural armor)
            * Description: A hunched, long-limbed goblin-like creature with a narrow skull, pointed ears, and dirt-stained skin.
            * Abilities: Burrow, pack ambush, swarm tactics

## Notes on Use
    * Adapt HP, damage, and armor values to your system.
